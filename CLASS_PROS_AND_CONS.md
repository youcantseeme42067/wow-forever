# World of Warcraft class pros and cons

Scope: **retail World of Warcraft**, reviewed 3 November 2026. The repository name is not treated as a game edition. Classic and other editions can have different classes, roles, and mechanics.

This is a playstyle comparison, not a current damage ranking. Roles and broad class identities follow Blizzard's class guides; the pros and cons are practical judgments about those features. Actual difficulty, mobility, survivability, and performance depend on specialization, talents, encounters, and patches. A class having several roles does not mean one specialization performs all of them simultaneously.

DPS means damage dealer.

| Class | Roles | Pros | Cons / tradeoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Tank, melee DPS | Heavy armor and a direct weapon-focused fantasy; can switch between protecting allies and dealing damage. | No healer specialization or ranged DPS option; melee positioning and managing rage are central to play. |
| Paladin | Tank, healer, melee DPS | Covers all three group roles; combines armored combat with healing and protection. | No ranged DPS specialization; getting value from support abilities requires attention to allies as well as enemies. |
| Hunter | DPS: ranged or melee, depending on specialization | Pets help with solo questing; offers a choice between ranged weapons and close combat. | Cannot switch to a tank or healer specialization; pet-based builds add a companion to manage. |
| Rogue | Melee DPS | Stealth enables deliberate openings; combo attacks reward planning and tactical combat. | No tank or healer specialization; requires close combat and attention to energy, finishers, and when to disengage. |
| Priest | Healer, ranged DPS | Can choose between healing allies and Shadow damage; suits a protective or dark spellcaster fantasy. | No tank or melee DPS specialization; switching from healing to damage still means learning a different specialization. |
| Shaman | Healer, ranged DPS, melee DPS | Broad choice of combat styles; elemental magic and totems provide varied ways to support or control a fight. | No tank specialization in retail; choosing and placing situational totems adds decisions beyond the main damage or healing loop. |
| Mage | Ranged DPS | Multiple spellcasting styles; crowd control, area attacks, and convenient travel magic. | No tank or healer specialization; light armor makes positioning and keeping enemies away important. |
| Warlock | Ranged DPS | Demon companions support the caster; offers damage-over-time, demon-commanding, and destructive spellcasting themes. | No tank or healer specialization; pet use and ongoing effects can add management, depending on the build. |
| Monk | Tank, healer, melee DPS | All three group roles in one class; distinctive martial arts and brewing themes. | No ranged DPS specialization; changing roles involves learning substantially different combat mechanics. |
| Druid | Tank, healer, melee DPS, ranged DPS | Widest role coverage; shapeshifting supports varied combat and travel preferences. | Multiple forms and role-specific toolkits can be a lot to learn; one specialization cannot replace a full specialist in every role at once. |
| Demon Hunter | Tank, DPS | Distinctive transformation fantasy and agile movement; offers both tanking and damage roles. | No healer specialization; movement abilities require care around hazards and unwanted enemies. |
| Death Knight | Tank, melee DPS | Armored undead-knight fantasy; can choose between tanking and damage. | No healer or ranged DPS specialization; less suitable if you want to avoid close combat. |
| Evoker | Healer, ranged DPS | Draconic magic, mobility, and empowered spells offer a distinctive caster experience. | No tank specialization; moderate spell range and charging empowered abilities make positioning and timing important. |

## How to narrow the choice

1. Pick the role you actually want to play: tank, healer, or DPS.
2. Decide whether melee, ranged combat, pets, or spellcasting sounds enjoyable.
3. Choose two or three appealing class fantasies.
4. Try a specialization from each before committing. Judge the repeated combat loop, not just a highlight video or a temporary tier ranking.
